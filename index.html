The Nature
of Being.

What exists beyond the limits of human perception? This course navigates the ultimate mysteries of existence, examining the elusive nature of space, the flow of time, and the architecture of the mind.

Access Syllabus
Phase 01

Ontology & Possible Worlds

We begin with the fundamental question: what is there? Explore the boundaries of existence and the mind-bending logic of alternate realities.

Phase 02

The Consciousness Enigma

Is the mind simply a biological machine, or something immaterial? Engage with thought experiments like the Chinese Room to test the limits of physicalism.

Phase 03

Time & Personal Identity

Examine the illusion of the flowing present. If your cells completely replace themselves, what makes you the identical person across the span of time?
