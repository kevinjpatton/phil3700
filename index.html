The Nature
of reality

What can science tell us about reality? Is time real? What exists? We will explore these questions, and more!

Access Syllabus
Phase 01

Space

We begin by trying to figure out what it is for objects to exist in space, and what sorts of philosophical implications follow.

Phase 02

Time

We continue on by trying to determine if time is an illusion, how objects can change over time, and if time travel is possible.

Phase 03

Reality

We close by examining how it is we come to know anything about the world, and reflect on the previous topics to see if epistemology can inform metaphysics.
